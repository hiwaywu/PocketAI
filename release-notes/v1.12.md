Browser upgrade, ad blocking, and no more untranslated Chinese.

**Browser**
- Fixed oversized, cut-off text: pages no longer multiply text by the phone's font size. Adjust with ⋮ → Text size.
- Tabs: tab counter button next to the address bar, tab list, new/close tab. Open tabs are remembered.
- Bookmarks: ☆ in the ⋮ menu, Bookmarks list, and bookmark tiles on the new-tab page (long-press to open in a new tab or remove).
- Tapping the address selects all of it so you can type over it; ✕ clears it.
- ⋮ menu: forward, bookmark, reload/stop, new tab, bookmarks, find in page, share, copy link, open in another browser, desktop site, block ads, text size.
- Links tapped in the chat open in a new tab.
- Asking about an article now sends only the article itself (like Firefox Reader View): no menus, sidebars or "other headlines" mixed into answers, and far fewer tokens (an RTHK article went from 3,326 to 221 characters).

**Ad blocking (⋮ → Block ads, on by default)**
- Blocks ~78,000 known ad and tracker servers (public list, updated weekly) and hides ad boxes on pages, including Reddit promoted posts and Google sponsored results.
- "Please allow ads / turn off your ad blocker" popups (e.g. hk01.com) and "You're using an adblocker" overlays (e.g. NY Daily News) are removed automatically and scrolling is turned back on.
- Empty reserved ad boxes (e.g. SCMP's gray boxes) and sticky bottom ads are hidden.

**Replies**
- The AI answers in your language (the words you type, even just "Summary") even when the source is in another language; foreign text is translated, with the original in brackets only when it matters.
- Better summaries: written like a journalist briefing a friend instead of a sentence-by-sentence translation — what happened and why it matters first, then the key details (who, numbers, dates, quotes), then what's still unclear.

Installs over 1.4–1.11 and keeps your chats.

The browser now comes with **Bypass Paywalls Clean**.

**Bypass Paywalls Clean**
- Installed automatically the first time you open the browser after updating, next to uBlock Origin. It gets past the paywalls of the news sites on its list (NYT, WSJ, FT, Bloomberg, The Economist and many more), so the full article loads and the message bar sends the AI the whole story, not just the teaser.
- ⋮ → **Add-ons** shows it as *Bypass Paywalls Clean · paywalls*: switch it off for a while, open its settings (choose sites, add custom sites), or remove it. If you remove it, it stays removed.
- It keeps its list of sites up to date by itself. For a newer version of the add-on, paste a direct .xpi link from its gitflic page into ⋮ → Add-ons.

Installs over 2.0 and keeps your chats, tabs, bookmarks and other add-ons.

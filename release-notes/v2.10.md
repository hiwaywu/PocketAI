Reads the whole YouTube transcript, not just the lines on screen.

**YouTube transcripts**
- With the transcript open, the Pocket AI button used to read only the lines you could see (about a minute of a 21-minute video). It now scrolls all the way through YouTube's transcript, by swiping it like a finger when the list ignores the normal scroll, and reads every line. You'll see it scroll; a long video takes a little while ("Reading the transcript…").
- It finds the transcript by its time stamps, whether YouTube shows them as "1:49" or reads them out as "1 minute, 49 seconds".
- If a transcript is already showing, it never taps anything else.
- Also in this version (from 2.9): on a video's page the button opens the description and taps "Show transcript" itself, and if that fails it finds the video on YouTube by its title and gives the AI the exact link.

If the button stops responding after this update, turn **Ask Pocket AI shortcut** off and on again in Accessibility settings (it now also needs to be able to swipe).

Installs over 2.2–2.9 and keeps your chats.

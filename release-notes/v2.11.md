YouTube transcripts, take three: read from the start to the end, with a backup.

**Reading the YouTube app's transcript**
- YouTube opens its transcript at the point the video is playing and keeps jumping to follow it. The Pocket AI button now pauses the video, scrolls up to the start of the transcript, then down to the end, collecting every line, sorted by time.
- If YouTube's list says it scrolled but didn't move, Pocket AI notices that nothing new appeared and switches to swiping it like a finger.
- It checks the transcript against the video's length (the player's "0:54 / 2:24"). If it's still incomplete, Pocket AI finds the video on YouTube and tells the AI to fetch the whole transcript from that link before answering, instead of searching news sites.

**See what happened**
- Tap "Read … characters" in the panel: under the text, "How it went" says what Pocket AI did, e.g. "paused the video; up: swipe moved ×3; down: swipe moved ×20; 212 lines, 0:00–21:20 of 21:25 (complete)". If transcripts still don't work, a screenshot of that line shows exactly where it stops.

YouTube comments: read them all and say what people think.

**Comments in the YouTube app**
- Open a video's **Comments**, then tap the Pocket AI button. It scrolls through the comments itself ("Reading the comments…") and reads up to 150 of them in YouTube's "Top comments" order, each with its likes and number of replies.
- Ask "What do the comments say?" (or anything else) and the AI sums up the mood and the main reactions, including the disagreements.
- Usernames are left out; they aren't needed and keep names out of the chat.
- Tap "Read … characters" to see the comments it read, and "How it went" for how many.

Transcript reading from 2.11 is unchanged: with the transcript or the video page open, the button reads the transcript.

Installs over 2.2–2.11 and keeps your chats.

YouTube transcript fix based on what the YouTube app actually shows.

**Fix**
- The YouTube app labels its transcript lines "5 minutes, 42 seconds two legs on…", but also labels the player's clock "5 minutes 58 seconds elapsed of 14 minutes 36 seconds". Pocket AI took the clock for a transcript line too, so it could latch onto the wrong part of the screen and only get the lines you could see. It now ignores the player's clock and finds the transcript's own list.

**If you're updating from 2.10 or earlier, this also brings:**
- Pausing the video, scrolling the transcript up to the start and down to the end (2.11), and a check against the video's length.
- YouTube comments reading (2.12), and a plain answer when YouTube hides the comments' text.
- The button never reads Pocket AI's own panel (2.13), and **Copy details** in "Read … characters" to send a report when something doesn't work.

Installs over 2.2–2.13 and keeps your chats.

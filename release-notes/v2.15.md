The Pocket AI button reads the right window, and the Reddit app works like sharing a link.

**Fixes**
- In the Reddit app the button read nothing ("Couldn't read this screen"): an app can have an empty window marked as the active one, and Pocket AI read that. It now reads the app window with the most text.
- **Copy details** works in every app now (before, only YouTube). When the button can't read something, tap "Details" (or "Read … characters") → **Copy details** and paste it to whoever is fixing Pocket AI.

**Reddit app**
- Tap the button on a post and Pocket AI finds that thread on reddit.com by its title ("Reading the Reddit thread…"), then reads all of it, just like sharing the link: the post, the article it links to, and the top comments with their upvotes. Ask "What is this thread about?" or "What are people saying?".

Installs over 2.2–2.14 and keeps your chats.

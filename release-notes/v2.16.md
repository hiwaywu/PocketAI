**The floating Pocket AI button**
- Pocket AI now draws its own round button (blue, with the chat bubble) over other apps:
  - **Tap**: ask about what's on the screen.
  - **Hold**: hide it — for 1 hour, or until you turn it back on.
  - **Drag**: move it; it snaps to the nearest edge and stays where you put it.
- It steps aside while the Pocket AI panel is open, so it no longer covers the panel's ✕.
- Bring it back: Pocket AI → Settings → *Floating Pocket AI button*, or the **Pocket AI button** Quick Settings tile (Settings → *Add show/hide to Quick Settings* adds it for you).

**Seeing two round buttons?** Android keeps its own accessibility button too. To remove it: Accessibility settings → Ask Pocket AI → ⚙ next to "Tap Accessibility button" → Location → "Swipe up with 2 fingers". Pocket AI stays on, and that two-finger swipe also asks Pocket AI.

Installs over 2.2–2.15 and keeps your chats.

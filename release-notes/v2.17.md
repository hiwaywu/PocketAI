**Floating Pocket AI button**
- **Hold** it: it slides into the side edge and leaves a small tab showing, like Android's own button.
- **Tap the tab**: it slides back out (tap again to ask Pocket AI).
- **Drag the tab** out to put it anywhere.
- **Hold the tab** for the full hide options: hide for 1 hour, or until you turn it back on.
- It stays tucked when you switch apps, until you tap it out.

Installs over 2.2–2.16 and keeps your chats.

One Pocket AI button instead of two.

- Pocket AI no longer asks Android for its accessibility button. The round Pocket AI bubble is the only button:
  tap to ask, hold to tuck it into the side, drag to move.
- Turning it on is now a plain on/off switch: Accessibility settings → Installed apps → Ask Pocket AI. No shortcut to pick.
- **After updating:** Android's own button stays until you remove it once: Accessibility settings → Installed apps →
  Ask Pocket AI → turn off its **shortcut**, and keep Ask Pocket AI itself on. (Until then, tapping Android's button
  switches Pocket AI off.) Pocket AI's Settings shows this reminder while that shortcut is still there.

Installs over 2.2–2.17 and keeps your chats.

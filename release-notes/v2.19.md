The Pocket AI button now reads YouTube videos the way sharing does.

- **In the YouTube app:** tap the Pocket AI button on a video. Pocket AI finds that video from its title and reads
  it from YouTube's website, with the whole transcript, the same way Share → Pocket AI does (which works). It takes a
  few seconds ("Reading the video…").
- It no longer taps around in the YouTube app (opening the description, pausing, scrolling the transcript). That
  never got more than the first few seconds.
- **Comments:** Pocket AI can't read YouTube comments. The app hides their text, so the AI now says so plainly
  instead of offering to read them from the link.

Installs over 2.2–2.18 and keeps your chats.

Better summaries, a much smaller app, and a lot of new chat features. The built-in browser is gone.

**Smaller: about 14 MB instead of ~100 MB**
- The browser (Firefox engine, uBlock Origin, Bypass Paywalls Clean) is removed. Links in chats now open in their own app when you have it (YouTube, Reddit…) or in a Chrome tab over the chat; Back returns to the chat.
- Works on any Android phone again, not just 64-bit ARM.

**Reddit threads**
- Pocket AI now reads a Reddit thread properly: the post with its score and % upvoted, the article or video it links to (read automatically, so you don't have to open it), and the top-voted comments with their upvotes and replies. Recommended posts and sidebars no longer get mixed in.
- Summaries say what was posted, **the vibe** of the comments, and **what people are saying**: the main reactions with upvotes, the pushback, useful facts or expert replies, and the top comment.

**Summaries in general**
- A bold TL;DR first, then the key details, shaped to the content: news, opinion, how-to, product, video or discussion.
- Articles are read like Firefox Reader View: just the story, plus the site, author and date (so the AI knows how old it is). Front pages keep all their headlines. Chinese sites like RTHK work too.
- No more "I'll open that page for you." at the top of answers.
- Chats started from a shared link are named after the page instead of the link.

**YouTube**
- Pocket AI opens YouTube's own transcript panel in the background, so summaries can cover what's actually said, not just the description. Key moments link to that point in the video.
- Also shows the upload date and views. If a video has no transcript, the summary says so.

**Chat**
- One-tap follow-ups under a link summary: "What's the pushback?", "Is this true?", "Key quotes", "More detail"…
- Long-press your message: **Copy** or **Edit and resend**.
- Under every reply: **Copy**, **Share**, **Read aloud** (the phone's own voice), **Regenerate**.
- **Voice typing**: the mic button appears when the message box is empty.
- A button to jump back to the newest message after scrolling up.
- Tables fit their content (times like 11:35 no longer break in two).

**Chat list**
- **Search** chats by title or anything said in them.
- **Pin**, **Rename**, **Share** or **Delete** a chat from its ⋮ menu. Chats are grouped by Today, Yesterday, Previous 7 days…
- Unfolded, the chat list stays open beside the chat (☰ hides it).

**Reinstall needed once:** 2.2 is signed with a new release key, so it can't install over 2.1. Uninstall the old Pocket AI first, then install 2.2. Uninstalling removes the chats and saved APIs stored on the phone, so note your API addresses and keys before you uninstall. Later updates install over 2.2 normally.

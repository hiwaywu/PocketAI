Pocket AI can now do simple things on your phone, and summaries of videos and Reddit threads are better.

- **Alarms, timers and calendar:** ask "wake me at 6:30 on weekdays", "timer 10 minutes", "dentist Thursday 3pm,
  remind me an hour before" or "what's on tomorrow?". Each action shows a card in the chat: tap it to open Clock or
  Calendar; calendar cards have **Undo**. It can also remove events ("remove the dentist") and turn alarms off.
  Calendar access is asked the first time. Phone alarms have no dates, so for a day further away it offers a calendar
  reminder instead. Settings → **Phone actions** turns it off.
- **YouTube summaries** now follow the video in order, start to end, with a time link for each part and who says what.
- **Reddit summaries** say what the post is about (the linked article, or the post's picture, which the AI can now
  see) and then what people are saying: the main reactions, disagreements and best comments.
- **Pocket AI button:** when an app hides its text (the Reddit app did), the button reads the text from a screenshot of
  the app instead, on the phone.

Installs over 2.2–2.19 and keeps your chats.

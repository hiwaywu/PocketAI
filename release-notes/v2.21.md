Reddit threads you share are read more fully.

- The AI now gets about twice as many comments from a thread, and is told to give the comments at least as much room
  as the linked article.
- The "Read: reddit.com" line under your question now says what was read, e.g. "Read: reddit.com · 142 comments +
  gothamist.com". If it says "no comments read", Reddit refused the comments; send a screenshot.
- Posts that paste part of a news story with its link now have the whole story read, not just the pasted part. If
  the story's site can't be opened, the AI looks up the same story elsewhere and reads that.
- **Shared Reddit links work again properly.** Reddit started refusing apps that download its pages directly, so
  Pocket AI was quietly falling back to a bare copy of the page (a few comments, no article). It now opens the thread
  in a hidden browser on your phone, loads more comments like you would by scrolling, and reads the post, the linked
  article and the comments from there.
- **Settings → Reddit → Sign in to Reddit:** Reddit often hides comments from apps that aren't signed in. Sign in
  once on Reddit's own page (email and password; Google sign-in may not work there), and shared threads are read as
  you, comments included. "Sign out of Reddit" removes it.
- If Reddit refuses the usual way of reading a thread, Pocket AI tries a second Reddit address before falling back to
  the web page (which only has the first few comments).

Installs over 2.2–2.20 and keeps your chats.

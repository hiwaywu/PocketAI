Cleaner summaries, and the keyboard gets out of the way.

**Cleaner summaries**
- No more labels like "TL;DR:", "The post:", "The vibe:" or "Why it matters:" at the start of paragraphs. A summary now opens with one or two plain sentences, then a few bullets with the key details, and ends with a short closing line only when it adds something.
- Reddit threads: what was posted, then "What people are saying" as a few bullets, then one sentence on the overall mood. Upvote counts no longer follow every point; they're mentioned only when striking.
- YouTube videos: the main point, a few bullets, then "Key moments" with tappable times.

**Keyboard**
- After you send a message, the keyboard closes so the reply has the whole screen.

Installs over 2.2 and keeps your chats.

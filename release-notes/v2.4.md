Pocket AI in every app: a button that reads what's on your screen. Gemini keeps the side button.

**The Pocket AI button**
- Tap it in Firefox, Reddit, YouTube or any app, and a Pocket AI panel slides up over the app with what's on screen already read: one-tap "Summarize this", "What are people saying?", "Explain this"… or type or speak your own question.
- In browsers it also reads the page's link, so for Reddit and YouTube pages the AI can open the full thread or the transcript. Articles you're logged into come through as you see them.
- The answer appears in the panel. Tap ↗ to continue in the full app; tap outside the panel (or ✕) to go back to what you were doing. The chat is saved in your chat list.
- Tap "Read … characters" to see exactly what Pocket AI read.
- It reads the screen only when you tap the button, never in the background. Password fields are skipped, and some apps (like banking apps) block it.

**Turn it on (once)**
- Settings → *Pocket AI in other apps* → Accessibility settings → Installed apps → Ask Pocket AI → on, and choose the floating button as its shortcut.
- If Android says "Restricted setting": Settings → *App info* → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings, then try again. (Android asks this for apps not from the Play Store.)

**Ask about selected text**
- Select text in any app → ⋮ → **Ask Pocket AI**: explain, summarize or translate just that part.

Installs over 2.2 and 2.3 and keeps your chats.

Natural summaries, YouTube transcripts from the YouTube app, and a visible search key.

**Summaries written naturally**
- The AI no longer follows a set layout. It explains a page, thread or video the way you'd tell a friend in a chat: what it's about and why it matters, then the details worth knowing, as a couple of paragraphs or a few bullets, whichever fits. No "TL;DR:"-style labels.

**YouTube app + the Pocket AI button**
- Watching a video: tap the Pocket AI button and ask "Summarize this video". The AI finds the video on YouTube by its title and reads its transcript.
- If YouTube's "Show transcript" button is on screen, or the transcript panel is already open, the Pocket AI button taps it and scrolls through the whole transcript itself (you'll see it scroll, and "Reading the transcript…"). Then the AI has every line, straight from the YouTube app.

**Settings**
- The search engine's API key (e.g. DeepSeek) has a 👁 button to show or hide it, like the API key.

Installs over 2.2–2.4 and keeps your chats.

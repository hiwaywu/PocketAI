Fixes from the first test of the Pocket AI button.

**The button continues your conversation**
- Tapping the Pocket AI button again within 30 minutes continues the same chat: you see the earlier messages, and the new screen is attached to your next question, so the AI knows it's related.
- Tap **+** in the panel to start a fresh chat instead.
- After a question, "Read … characters" turns into "Sent … characters"; follow-ups in the same panel don't send the same screen again.

**YouTube app**
- The button now reads the video's title, channel and description. YouTube keeps them in hidden labels that 2.6 skipped (it only got the player's time, 83 characters).
- With the title, "Summarize this video" finds the video on YouTube and reads its transcript. The AI no longer asks you for the link.
- If the video is full screen there's no title to read: exit full screen first.

**Firefox**
- The page's address is found again (newer Firefox draws its address bar differently), so the panel shows the site, and the AI can open the full page when the screen isn't enough. If Firefox has hidden its toolbar while scrolling, scroll up a little first.

Installs over 2.2–2.6 and keeps your chats.

The Pocket AI button now knows when to continue a chat and when to start a new one.

**Same page → same chat; new page → new chat**
- Tap the button again on the same article, video or thread (even scrolled further) and the chat continues: the AI sees your earlier questions and knows it's related.
- Go to a different website, article, video or app and the button starts a new chat on its own, so old pages aren't sent again with every question (that cost extra tokens and could mix topics up).
- After 30 minutes it always starts fresh.
- If it guessed wrong, tap **↩ Continue last chat** to go back to the previous chat, or **+** to start a new one yourself.

**Firefox**
- The page's address is found in more cases (some browsers hide invisible marks around it).

Installs over 2.2–2.7 and keeps your chats.

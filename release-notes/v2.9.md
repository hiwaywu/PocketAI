**Pocket AI button in the YouTube app**
- On a video's page, the button now opens the description itself (the "...more" under the title), taps **Show transcript**, and scrolls through YouTube's transcript to read all of it ("Opening the transcript…", then "Reading the transcript…"). You'll see YouTube's transcript panel open while it reads.
- If the description or transcript is already open, it goes straight to reading.
- If the transcript can't be opened, Pocket AI finds the video on YouTube by its title ("Finding the video…") and gives the AI that exact video's link, so it reads the video's own transcript instead of searching news sites about it.
- The video's link also tells the button when you've moved to another video (new chat) or are still on the same one (same chat).

Installs over 2.2–2.8 and keeps your chats.
